The Heart

Heart

The tireless pump

A strong muscle that pumps blood all around your body, carrying oxygen and food to every cell.

Key facts

◇ Size
About the size of your fist
♙ Weight
250–350 g
⌁ Daily
Beats about 100,000 times
⌖ Location
In your chest, a little to the left
◈ Function
Pumps blood around your body

How it worksTiny electrical signals tell it when to squeeze, so every beat happens in the right order.

Did you knowIt beats roughly 2.5 billion times in an average lifetime, and starts before you are born.
